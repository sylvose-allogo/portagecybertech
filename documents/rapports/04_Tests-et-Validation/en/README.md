English test reports are provided as HTML, Word and PDF:

- `oauth2-contract-test-report` — Postman contract-test report.
- `unit-test-results` — historical Maven unit-test results.
- `integration-test-results` — historical Maven integration and contract
  test results.

Dated results refer only to their recorded executions and do not establish
the current repository status. Collections, environments, scripts and
captures remain in `../Postman/`; raw historical results remain in
`../Rapports-historiques/`. Profiles named Int and Prod simulate localhost
and do not certify remote environments.
